About

Instrument 18 of Infra Atlas. The cross-cloud data-transfer cost map — what AWS, Azure and Google Cloud charge to move a gigabyte: out to the internet, between zones and regions, and through a NAT gateway or a private interconnect. Each route shows the price ladder inline; click any cloud for the note and the source.

Sources & method

Every rate is a published list price, curated from the providers’ official pricing pages — AWS EC2, VPC and Direct Connect; Azure bandwidth, NAT Gateway and ExpressRoute; Google Cloud network, NAT and Interconnect. Source URLs are cited in each cell’s drawer. Pricing pages render dynamically and change without notice — this is a snapshot, verified 2026-05-19, not a live feed. List price, never the invoiced price. Spot a stale rate? Open an issue.
