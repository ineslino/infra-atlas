On keeping it free

Support the atlas.

Infra Atlas is free to read, vendor-neutral, and beholden to nobody — because nobody pays for a place in it. That independence costs a little to keep. If the atlas has saved you time, you can help cover it.

The ask From the cartographer

Infra Atlas is made by one person, in the evenings, in the EU. No investors, no ads, no vendor money, and nothing to sell you — which is the whole point: with nothing to sell, nothing pulls the data off-true.

It does cost a little to run — a domain, hosting, and the privacy-respecting analytics that keep it measurable. If the atlas saved you a morning of grepping vendor documentation across five open tabs, you can cover a slice of that.

There is no paywall, and there never will be. €0 to read, always. A tip is a thank-you, not a toll — give once if the atlas earned it, and nothing if it didn't.

The honest part Where it goes

What your support pays for

  • The domain and the hosting bill.
  • Privacy-respecting analytics — cookieless, no third-party tracking.
  • A coffee for the evenings spent keeping the data current.

What it will never pay for

  • A paywall. The atlas is €0 to read, always.
  • Tiers, perks or "supporter" features — it stays a plain tip jar.
  • A vendor's place in the data. Neutrality is not for sale.

Donations cover running costs and a coffee — not a salary. That is the honest size of it, and it is enough.
