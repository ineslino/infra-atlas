# Phase 7 — Risks & anti-patterns

Explicit "do not" list. Each would either break the editorial brand, the
privacy promise, or the donations-only model. Recorded so future pressure
doesn't relitigate them.

| # | Do NOT | Why |
|---|--------|-----|
| 1 | **Newsletter popups, modal interrupts, scroll/exit overlays.** | The site's whole appeal is a calm, dense reference. An interrupt is the single most off-brand thing possible — it signals "marketing site," not "instrument." |
| 2 | **Exit-intent donation popups.** | Donations convert on *gratitude*, not ambush. An exit popup converts annoyance into a worse opinion of the project. |
| 3 | **AI-generated SEO content-farm pages.** | Thin generated pages contradict "every claim sourced, asterisks intact" and risk a Google quality penalty that would bury the real instruments. |
| 4 | **Paid placements / sponsored vendor sections / vendor-favouring "comparison" pages.** | Vendor neutrality *is* the product. One paid row and the entire cross-vendor comparison is worthless. |
| 5 | **Engagement-bait social posts** ("AWS or Azure?? 🤔 RT to vote!"). | Off-voice (editorial, dry), attracts the wrong audience, and trains the feed to expect noise. |
| 6 | **NFTs, a token, "supporter badge" gimmicks.** | Brand poison for a serious infra-engineering audience; turns a trust project into a stunt. |
| 7 | **Removing or paywalling existing free data — ever, under any growth pressure.** | "€0 to read" is a homepage promise and the core of the positioning. Breaking it once ends the trust permanently. |
| 8 | **Selling user data or adding third-party trackers — ever.** | The audience is privacy-conscious developers; the analytics choice (cookieless, EU) is itself a trust signal. One tracker undoes it. |
| 9 | **SaaS drift — accounts, login, a "Pro tier", "premium" anything.** | Donations are the *only* model (mission hard rule). A tier turns a reference into a product and forks the data into haves/have-nots. |
| 10 | **Conference booths or paid sponsorships (in either direction) before donations sustain them.** | Wrong cost structure for a donation-funded solo project; spend follows revenue, not hope. |
| 11 | **Hiring "growth" contractors who pitch "we'll 10x your traffic".** | Wrong reference class — that playbook is venture-SaaS funnel-hacking; it will produce off-voice content and vanity metrics. |
| 12 | **Dark-pattern donation framing** — fake urgency, guilt, fake scarcity, "only 3 supporters left". | The donation ask is editorial and honest (`donations.md` §5a/5e). A manipulated ask earns a one-time euro and loses a reader. |
| 13 | **Buying followers / stars / engagement.** | Hollow social proof that infra engineers detect instantly; real slow growth beats fake fast growth for a trust project. |

**The test for any future tactic:** does it keep the site calm, free, neutral,
privacy-respecting, and editorial? If not, it belongs on this list.
