Reading the "stack" column. Most rows compare a component within a wider deployment — Loki is the logs component of the Grafana stack, Elasticsearch is the storage tier of ELK, etc. A ✓ means the stack covers that signal natively; ◐ means it covers it via a peripheral or with caveats; ✗ means you would pair the stack with something else for that signal. The licence and storage rows describe the open-source distribution; commercial editions may differ. The companion Observability Matrix covers the cloud-native and managed-platform options.

Scope

Open-source stacks you deploy and operate yourself. Excludes vendor-hosted versions (Grafana Cloud, Elastic Cloud, Aiven Loki, etc.) — those live in the Observability Matrix Section B. Commercial extensions (Elastic paid tier, Grafana Enterprise) are noted in cell footnotes only where the OSS distribution alone is materially limited.

Sources

Every cell cites the project’s official documentation. Licences change — Elastic and Grafana have both made non-trivial changes since 2021. Verify the current licence on the project repo before adopting. Spot an error? Open an issue →

What’s not here

Newer all-in-one OSS APMs (SigNoz, HyperDX, OpenObserve) — strong candidates but each has fewer than 3 years of mainstream production references at the time of writing. Operational burden estimates (storage cost, ops headcount) — too deployment-specific to compare meaningfully. See also: Cloud-native vs managed vs self-hosted decision.
