What would this workload cost elsewhere? Pick an instance; the comparator pulls its closest equivalent on every other cloud — the same transparent scoring as the Equivalent-SKU Finder — and lines up the monthly bill: compute hours plus internet egress, from the live catalogues and the Egress Cost Map. These are list prices, not a quote. Compute is the hourly on-demand list price (Linux; USD, except OVHcloud in EUR) times your hours — spot, reserved, savings plans and negotiated rates are out of scope, and regional price uplifts are not modelled (the region picker checks availability, not price). Egress walks the internet-egress tier ladder curated in the Egress Cost Map for AWS, Azure and GCP; OCI and OVHcloud egress is not mapped there yet, so their totals are compute-only. GCP publishes no per-size list prices in the Atlas dataset, so its compute line is unavailable. EUR and USD totals are not converted — compare like with like.
