The cheapest per-GB tier is rarely the cheapest bill. Cold and archive tiers trade their low storage price for a retrieval fee, a minimum-duration penalty (delete early and you pay the rest anyway), and a restore wait measured in hours. The numbers below are list prices in USD for a representative US region (S3 N. Virginia, Azure East US, GCS us-central1; OCI and OVH price uniformly; OVH is shown in USD from its US price page, EU customers are billed in EUR). GCS and OVHcloud publish per-GiB rates (binary, 230); shown here converted to per-GB to align the column — a ~7% basis difference the headline price hides. The marks score what actually helps or bites: instant access vs a mandatory restore, and free vs billed egress and requests. Storage prices and durations stay descriptive (≈) — a fact, not a virtue. Region, redundancy (LRS vs multi-region) and volume tiers move every number; read the cell's source before you commit one to a slide.

About

The object-storage cost layer of Infra Atlas — S3, Azure Blob, Google Cloud Storage, OCI Object Storage and OVHcloud, mapped across the dimensions that decide the real bill, not just the per-GB headline. Click any cell for the note and the source URL. Scope is the public object-storage classes; block and file storage are out of scope.

Sources

Every cell is read off the vendor’s own pricing and docs pages, cited in the cell’s drawer. Prices shift with region, redundancy and volume, and the vendors restructure tiers — this is a curated snapshot, verified 2026-06-12 and reviewed roughly quarterly (the CI nags at 120 days). If you spot a stale price, open an issue. For data-transfer costs in depth, see the Egress Cost Map.
